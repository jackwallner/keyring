PURPOSE
Vehicle Maintenance Log is a shared service history for every vehicle a household owns: cars, trucks, motorcycles, RVs, boats, aircraft, ATVs and mowers. Families lose track of what's due and lose receipts across several vehicles and drivers. The app knows each vehicle's schedule, reminds whoever is responsible, and keeps a record a buyer can trust. No account or login: data lives on the device and in the user's own iCloud.

HOW TO REVIEW (about 2 minutes, no credentials needed)
1. Tap Continue through four intro pages. On the last page, "Get Started" (above the price) enters the free app without buying.
2. Garage tab, tap + and choose Car. Enter the sample VIN 1HGCM82633A004352 (fills in a 2003 Honda Accord) or type any make and model, add the odometer, answer "When were these last done?" and tap "Add to garage". A schedule is built and each job counts down by date and miles.
3. On the vehicle page tap "Log service", tick a job, add a cost and save: that countdown restarts. Tap "Fill-up" to log fuel (mpg shows after two full tanks).
4. Scroll to Explore for recalls, value, parts, upgrades, clubs and service nearby.
Choosing Boat or Aircraft instead gives a different schedule in engine hours.

WHAT MAKES IT DISTINCT (4.3)
This is our only vehicle or maintenance app and it is not a re-skin of another app. Built for it:
- A maintenance catalog for 18 vehicle kinds and their powertrains (gas, diesel, hybrid, plug-in, electric, outboard, inboard, sterndrive, piston and turbine aircraft), counted in miles, kilometers or engine hours, plus a second meter such as an RV's generator.
- A whole-household garage shared through CloudKit, so each family member logs from their own iPhone with no account.
- On-device reading of receipts, fuel pumps, odometers and VINs.
- Recall matching: a recall is closed when a logged repair, receipt or note shows the fix.
- Storage mode that pauses reminders, a trip check of what comes due on a long drive, a mod build list, and a Service Record PDF for a buyer.

FAMILY SHARING
Garage tab, people button at top left: an iCloud invite link and QR code (CKShare). Joining needs a second iCloud account on another device. Sharing is private, between people the owner invites. There is no public feed, profile, chat or content from strangers, so the age rating declares no user-generated content.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Apple iCloud (CloudKit): sync and family sharing.
- NHTSA public APIs (vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov, api.nhtsa.gov): VIN decoding and US safety recalls. Not affiliated with NHTSA.
- RevenueCat: processes in-app purchases.
- Apple Maps: "Service nearby" opens a Maps search.
- Links that open in Safari: price guides (Kelley Blue Book, Edmunds, J.D. Power or the local guide), parts, owners clubs. The app never shows a third-party value as its own. No affiliate tags.
Receipt, pump, odometer and VIN reading run on the device. No analytics or ad SDKs.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
Features are the same everywhere. Distance units, the make list, price-guide links and the inspection name follow the region. Recall lookups use NHTSA (US data); in Canada and the UK the app also links to the national recall site.

PERMISSIONS
Camera (scan a VIN, odometer, pump, receipt or invite code) and Photos (attach receipts) are asked on first use. Notifications (maintenance and recall reminders) are asked after the first vehicle is added.

IN-APP PURCHASES
Two vehicles with all logging, fill-ups, reminders, recalls, sharing and CSV export are free. Pro (monthly or yearly subscription with a one-week free trial, or lifetime) adds unlimited vehicles, the Service Record PDF, receipt and pump auto-fill, and spending insights. The paywall opens on adding a third vehicle, from the Pro row in Settings, or on the last intro page. Restore and Terms/Privacy links are on the paywall.

Schedules are general guidance; the app says to follow the owner's manual. It is not an official aircraft or vessel logbook and not a regulated service.
