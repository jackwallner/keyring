Vehicle Maintenance Log keeps one shared service history for every vehicle your family owns. The truck, your partner's SUV, the teen's first car, the boat, the motorcycle, the camper, the plane and the mower all live in one garage, and each one tells you what's due next.

ONE GARAGE, THE WHOLE FAMILY
Invite anyone with a link or a QR code. Everyone logs from their own iPhone, every phone shows the same history, and nobody needs an account. Reminders are set on each phone, so one oil change doesn't ping the whole house.

KNOWS WHAT'S DUE
Every vehicle starts with a recommended schedule for its kind and powertrain: oil, tires, brakes, filters, fluids, inspections, winterizing, impellers, annuals. Tell the app when each job was last done and it counts down by date and by miles, kilometers or engine hours, using how much you actually drive.

LOG IN SECONDS
Tick what was done, add the mileage, cost and shop, and snap the receipt. Every checked job restarts its own countdown. Any typed field, from the license plate and VIN to odometer readings and notes, can be filled from a photo.

FILL-UPS AND REAL MPG
Log every fill-up beside the service history. Snap the odometer and the reading fills in. With Pro, snap the pump display and the gallons, price per gallon, total and grade fill in, read right on your iPhone. Fuel economy is worked out from full tank to full tank, in mpg or L/100 km.

BRING YOUR HISTORY
Import a CSV from Fuelly, Drivvo, Simply Auto or your own spreadsheet. The app matches the columns, brings fill-ups in as fill-ups, and restarts each job's countdown from the last time it was done.

SEASONS, SALES AND SECOND METERS
Put the boat or the convertible in storage and reminders pause until it's back out. Track an RV's generator hours on their own schedule. When you sell, note the date and price and hand the buyer the full record.

REPAIRS AND RECALLS TOO
Log unplanned repairs alongside maintenance and see what each one really costs. The app watches NHTSA for new safety recalls on your cars and lets you know when one is issued. Recalls a dealer already fixed are spotted on saved receipts and notes.

EVERY KIND OF VEHICLE
Cars, SUVs, trucks, vans, motorcycles, scooters, motorhomes, campers, trailers, boats, sailboats, jet skis, aircraft, ATVs, snowmobiles, tractors and mowers.

RECALLS, VALUE AND PARTS
- Safety recalls from NHTSA for your year, make and model, with an alert when a new one is issued
- One tap to Kelley Blue Book, Edmunds, J.D. Power or your country's price guide, and a place to note what it's worth
- The tools and parts for the job that's due next
- Quick facts you always look up: oil type, tire size, wiper lengths, paint code

UPGRADES, CLUBS AND SERVICE NEARBY
Performance, handling and detailing upgrades owners actually recommend, with the honest tradeoffs. Owners clubs and forums for your make. Repair shops, mobile mechanics and mobile detailers near you.

A RECORD WORTH HANDING OVER
Search and filter the whole history, export it to CSV, and with Pro print a clean Service Record PDF with every job and receipt for a buyer, a shop or the glovebox.

FREE FOR YOUR FIRST TWO
Two vehicles are free for good, with unlimited logging, fill-ups and mpg, reminders, recalls, family sharing and CSV export. Pro adds unlimited vehicles, the Service Record PDF, receipts and pump photos that fill themselves in, and spending insights.
