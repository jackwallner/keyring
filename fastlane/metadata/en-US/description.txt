Vehicle Maintenance Log keeps one shared service history and MPG log for every vehicle your family owns, and tells you what's due next. The truck, the teen's first car, the boat, the motorcycle, the camper, the plane and the mower live in one garage. Two vehicles are free, and nobody needs an account.

ONE GARAGE, THE WHOLE FAMILY
Invite anyone with a link or a QR code. Everyone logs from their own iPhone, every phone shows the same history, and nobody needs an account. Reminders are set per phone, so one oil change doesn't ping the whole house.

KNOWS WHAT'S DUE
Every vehicle starts with a recommended schedule for its kind and powertrain: oil, tires, brakes, filters, fluids, inspections, winterizing, impellers, annuals. It counts down by date and by miles, kilometers or engine hours, at the pace you actually drive.

LOG IN SECONDS
Tick what was done, add the mileage, cost and shop, and snap the receipt. Every checked job restarts its own countdown. Any typed field, from the plate and VIN to odometer readings and notes, can be filled from a photo.

FILL-UPS AND REAL MPG
Log fill-ups beside the service history. Snap the odometer and the reading fills in. With Pro, snap the pump and the gallons, price, total and grade fill in. Fuel economy runs full tank to full tank, in mpg or L/100 km.

BRING YOUR HISTORY
Import a CSV from another maintenance or fuel app, or your own spreadsheet. Fill-ups come in as fill-ups, and each job's countdown restarts from the last time it was done.

SEASONS, SALES AND SECOND METERS
Put the boat in storage and its reminders pause. Track an RV's generator hours on their own schedule. When you sell, hand the buyer the full record.

REPAIRS AND RECALLS
Log unplanned repairs alongside maintenance. The app checks NHTSA's public database for safety recalls on your cars and lets you know when a new one is issued. Recalls already fixed are spotted on saved receipts and notes.

MODS, EVENTS AND PAPERWORK
Keep a build list of every mod with its parts and cost. Log a track day or a trail ride and see which jobs it brings due. Check what comes due before a long trip, and keep the title and insurance card with the vehicle.

EVERY KIND OF VEHICLE
Cars, SUVs, trucks, vans, motorcycles, scooters, e-bikes, golf carts, motorhomes, campers, trailers, boats, sailboats, jet skis, aircraft, ATVs, snowmobiles, tractors and mowers.

VALUE, PARTS, CLUBS AND SERVICE NEARBY
- One tap to your country's used-vehicle price guides, and a place to note what it's worth
- The tools and parts for the job that's due next
- Quick facts you always look up: oil type, tire size, wiper lengths, paint code
- Upgrades worth doing, with the honest tradeoffs, and owners clubs for your make
- Repair shops, mobile mechanics and detailers near you in Apple Maps

A RECORD WORTH HANDING OVER
Search the whole history, export it to CSV, and with Pro print a clean Service Record PDF with every job and receipt for a buyer, a shop or the glovebox.

FREE FOR YOUR FIRST TWO
Two vehicles are free for good, with unlimited logging, fill-ups and mpg, reminders, recalls, family sharing and CSV export. Pro adds unlimited vehicles, the Service Record PDF, receipts and pump photos that fill themselves in, and spending insights.
