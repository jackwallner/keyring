Questions

What is the best app to track car maintenance for the whole family?

Keyring keeps one shared garage for every vehicle you own. Everyone you invite logs from their own iPhone, sees the same history, and gets their own reminders, with no account to create.

Can I track a boat, motorcycle or airplane, not just cars?

Yes. Keyring has schedules for cars, trucks, SUVs, vans, motorcycles, scooters, motorhomes, campers, trailers, boats, sailboats, jet skis, aircraft, ATVs, snowmobiles, tractors and mowers. Boats, aircraft and small engines run on hours instead of miles.

How does Keyring know what maintenance is due?

Each vehicle starts with a recommended schedule for its kind and powertrain. Tell it when each job was last done and Keyring counts down by date and by mileage or hours, using how much you actually drive to estimate the date.

Can I print a service record when I sell my car?

Yes. The Service Record is a clean PDF with every job, the schedule and your receipts, ready to print or send to a buyer or shop. It is part of Keyring Pro.

Does Keyring show what my car is worth?

Keyring links straight to Kelley Blue Book, Edmunds and J.D. Power (or Aircraft Bluebook for planes) with your vehicle filled in, and lets you note the value you found. It does not show third-party values as its own.

Does it check for recalls?

Yes. For cars, trucks and motorcycles Keyring checks NHTSA's public recall database for your year, make and model and notifies you when a new one is issued. Log the dealer fix and it is marked done for everyone in the garage, and it shows on the Service Record.

Is Keyring free?

Two vehicles are free for good, with unlimited logging, reminders, recalls, family sharing and CSV export. Keyring Pro adds unlimited vehicles, the Service Record PDF, receipt auto-fill and spending insights. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region.

Do I need an account?

No. Your garage is stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, and sharing uses Apple's iCloud sharing.
