## Vehicle Maintenance Log & MPG

Vehicle Maintenance Log & MPG keeps one shared garage for every vehicle in the family, from cars and trucks to motorcycles, boats, campers, aircraft and mowers. Invite anyone with a link or QR code and each person logs from their own iPhone, sees the same history and keeps their own reminders. Each vehicle starts with a recommended schedule for its kind and powertrain, counted down by date and by miles, kilometers or engine hours at the pace you actually drive. It logs fill-ups and works out mpg or L/100 km from full tank to full tank, checks NHTSA recalls for cars, trucks and motorcycles, and prints a Service Record PDF with every job and receipt. You can import a CSV from another app and export to CSV any time. Two vehicles are free for good with unlimited logging, fill-ups, reminders, recalls, sharing and export, and Pro adds unlimited vehicles, the Service Record PDF, receipt and pump auto-fill and spending insights. There is no account and no ads. The trade-offs: it is new with a single rating, and the Service Record PDF is part of Pro.

## CARFAX Car Care

CARFAX Car Care is built around service history. Its listing describes a dashboard of what is due, service alerts, estimated value, repair cost estimates, ratings of service centers, safety recall notifications and mileage and fuel tracking. It has by far the largest audience of the five. Choose it if you want your car's history, recalls and local shop information in one place. The listing does not describe sharing a garage with family.

## Fuelly: MPG & Service Tracker

Fuelly tracks fuel economy and maintenance records, with charts for mpg, gas price and expenses, service reminders with presets such as oil changes and tire rotations, attachments for photos and PDFs, and sync with Fuelly.com. It supports many unit formats and CSV email reports. The latest update shown is June 2024. Choose it if you want your fuel history on the Fuelly website too.

## Drivvo - Car Expense Tracker

Drivvo logs fill-ups, expenses and service, calculates gas mileage and cost per mile, and sends reminders by mileage or date. Its listing also describes EV charging, nearby gas prices from its community, cars, motorcycles, trucks, buses and equipment with hour meters, and syncing between phone and computer. Choose it if what the vehicle costs to run is your main question.

## My Car - Vehicle Manager

My Car is a vehicle logbook, fuel log and expense tracker. Its listing describes receipt photos attached to events, mileage and trip logging, cost breakdowns, support for electric and hybrid vehicles, a web version, sharing vehicles with family or a team, and up to three vehicles free. Choose it if you want charts of ownership cost across fuel, parts and maintenance.

## What should you look for in a car maintenance app?

Reminders by date and mileage, a place to keep receipts, and fuel logging cover most needs. Recalls, an exportable record and sharing matter more as you add vehicles and drivers.

- Reminders that count down by miles and by date, not only one or the other.
- A way to bring your history in and take it out, such as CSV import and export.
- Recall alerts, so you hear about a safety notice without checking.
- A shared log if more than one person drives or services the vehicles.

## Which should you pick?

For a family garage with a service record, pick Vehicle Maintenance Log & MPG. For service history and shop information, pick CARFAX Car Care.

- Several drivers and vehicles, including a boat or motorcycle: Vehicle Maintenance Log & MPG.
- Service history, recalls and repair cost estimates: CARFAX Car Care.
- Fuel economy history on a website: Fuelly.
- Fuel, expenses and cost per mile: Drivvo.
- A logbook with web access and charts: My Car.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best app to track car maintenance on iPhone?

Vehicle Maintenance Log & MPG for a shared garage with schedules, fill-ups and a service record, CARFAX Car Care for service history and recalls, and Fuelly or Drivvo for fuel and expense tracking.

### Is there an app that tracks gas mileage and maintenance together?

Yes. Vehicle Maintenance Log & MPG, Fuelly, Drivvo, My Car and CARFAX Car Care all combine fuel logging with service reminders, according to their listings.

### Can one app track maintenance for the whole family's vehicles?

Yes. Vehicle Maintenance Log & MPG keeps one shared garage, and anyone you invite logs from their own iPhone and sees the same history, with reminders kept personal. My Car's listing also describes sharing vehicles with family or a team.

### Can I track a boat, motorcycle or tractor, not just cars?

Yes. Vehicle Maintenance Log & MPG has schedules for motorcycles, boats, aircraft, ATVs, tractors and mowers, with hours instead of miles where that fits. Drivvo's listing also mentions motorcycles, trucks, buses and equipment with hour meters.

### Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes, in Vehicle Maintenance Log & MPG. Import a CSV export from Fuelly, Drivvo, Simply Auto, aCar or your own spreadsheet and the countdowns start from your real history.

### Do I need an account to track maintenance?

Not for Vehicle Maintenance Log & MPG, which has no account and stores your garage on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. The other apps' listings are not explicit about accounts.
