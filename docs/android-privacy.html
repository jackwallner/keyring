Privacy Policy for Android

Vehicle Maintenance Log for Android keeps your garage on your phone. There is no account to create, nothing is sold, and there are no advertising trackers or analytics SDKs.

Your garage

Vehicles, schedules, service entries, fill-ups, meter readings and notes are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. The developer cannot read them. Family sharing and iPhone sync are not available on Android.

Lookups

To decode a VIN and check recalls, the app sends the VIN, or the year, make and model, to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's public APIs (vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov and api.nhtsa.gov). Nothing else about you is sent. Links to Amazon and nhtsa.gov open in your browser with the search or campaign in the address; what those sites collect is governed by their own policies.

Notifications

With your permission, reminders are scheduled on your device with Android's alarm service. No push server is involved.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. The app uses RevenueCat to confirm them. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, purchase records and simple counts of how often a purchase screen was shown. It does not receive your garage. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Deleting your data

Delete a vehicle or entry in the app and it is removed from your phone. To remove everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings, Apps, Maintenance Log. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

Children

Vehicle Maintenance Log is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
