Maintenance guidance

Recommended schedules are general guidance for each kind of vehicle. Your owner's manual, your manufacturer and a qualified mechanic or inspector come first, and for aircraft the applicable regulations and your maintenance records govern. Vehicle Maintenance Log is a record-keeping tool and does not certify airworthiness, roadworthiness or the completion of any work.

Recalls and values

Recall information comes from NHTSA and may be incomplete; confirm with a dealer using your VIN. Vehicle values are on the third-party sites the app links to and are not provided by the app.
